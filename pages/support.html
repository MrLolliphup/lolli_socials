Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I place an order? +

Go to Services, choose the service you need, enter the required details, and complete your order.

How do I fund my wallet? +

Open Wallet and select your preferred funding method. Payment options will be connected to the backend later.

How long does an order take? +

Delivery time depends on the service you purchase. You can track your order from the Orders page.

What if my order fails? +

Contact support with your order ID. Failed orders can be reviewed and handled by the support team.

Can I get a refund? +

Refund eligibility depends on the specific service and order status. Contact support for assistance.
